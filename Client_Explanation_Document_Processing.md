# AI Document Processing System - Client Explanation

## What This System Does

Your AI Document Processing System is a powerful tool that can **analyze and manipulate documents** using advanced artificial intelligence. Think of it as having a smart assistant that can:

1. **Answer questions** about your documents (like "What are the key points in this contract?")
2. **Edit and modify** documents (like "Add a new clause to this agreement")
3. **Create new documents** based on your existing files
4. **Merge multiple documents** into one
5. **Extract specific information** from large document collections

## How It Works - Simple Explanation

### Step 1: You Ask a Question or Request
- You type what you want to do with your documents
- The system understands whether you want **information** or want to **make changes**

### Step 2: Smart Document Search
- The system searches through all your uploaded documents
- It finds the most relevant documents for your request
- Uses advanced AI to understand the meaning, not just keywords

### Step 3: AI Processing
- **For Questions**: Uses a fast, cost-effective AI model to answer your questions
- **For Actions**: Uses a more powerful AI model to make changes to your documents

### Step 4: Results
- You get your answer or your modified documents
- Everything is saved so you can review it later

## Two Main Modes

### 📋 Question Answering Mode
**When you want to GET information from your documents**

Examples:
- "Summarize this contract"
- "What are the key points in this report?"
- "Find all mentions of 'budget' in these documents"
- "What does this legal document say about liability?"

**How it works:**
- Searches your documents quickly
- Uses document summaries for fast answers
- No need to download full files
- Fast and cost-effective

### 🔧 Action Performance Mode
**When you want to MAKE CHANGES to your documents**

Examples:
- "Edit this contract to add a new clause"
- "Create a new agreement based on this template"
- "Merge these two documents into one"
- "Rewrite this section to be more clear"

**How it works:**
- Downloads full document content
- Uses advanced AI for complex reasoning
- Can make actual changes to documents
- More powerful but takes a bit longer

## Quick Start Guide

**Desktop**: 
- Click the blue "Find Attorney" button in the top right, OR

**Mobile**:

1. Click "Advanced Assistant" for more detailed help
2. Upload relevant documents if needed
3. Get comprehensive legal guidance

### 📋 Tracking Your Requests

1. Click "My Requests" in the sidebar
2. See all attorneys you've contacted

4. View messages from attorneys

1. Click "Messages" in the My Space section
2. See unread count in red badge
3. Chat with attorneys who accepted your requests
4. Share documents and information

## Common Questions

### Q: How do I find an attorney?

**A**: Click the blue "Find Attorney" button in the top bar, or select "Find Attorney" from the sidebar. You can browse all attorneys or use filters to find specialists in your area.

### Q: What if an attorney doesn't respond?

**A**: Attorneys typically respond within 24-48 hours. If you don't hear back, you can send requests to other attorneys. There's no limit on requests!

### Q: Are AI consultations free?

**A**: Basic AI consultations are included with your account. Advanced features may require credits.

### Q: How do messages work?

**A**: Once an attorney accepts your request, a message thread opens. You'll see it in "Messages" with a red badge for unread messages.

### Q: Can I change my profile information?

**A**: Yes! Click "My Profile" anytime to update your information, location, or legal needs.

### Q: What are credits used for?

**A**: Credits are used for premium features like advanced AI analysis, document processing, and some attorney services.

### Q: Is my information secure?

**A**: Yes! All communications are encrypted and secure. Your privacy is our top priority.

### Q: Can I use this on my phone?

**A**: Absolutely! The interface is optimized for mobile with easy-to-tap buttons and smooth navigation.

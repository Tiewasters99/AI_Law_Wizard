# Docket Genie User Guide

## Overview

**Docket Genie** is a comprehensive PACER (Public Access to Court Electronic Records) integration feature designed for attorneys. It allows direct access to federal court records, case dockets, and court documents without leaving the AI Wizard platform.

## Key Features

- **PACER Authentication**: Secure login with your PACER credentials
- **Case Search**: Search federal court cases by multiple criteria
- **Docket Reports**: View complete docket entries with documents
- **Case Details**: Access comprehensive case information, parties, and attorneys
- **Document Download**: Download and analyze court documents
- **AI Integration**: Automatically analyze downloaded documents with AI

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

1. **Attorney Account**: Only attorneys can access Docket Genie
2. **PACER Account**: You must have an active PACER account
3. **PACER Credentials**: Username and password for authentication

### Accessing Docket Genie

1. Log in to AI Wizard with your attorney account
2. Navigate to **Court Integration > Docket Genie** in the sidebar
3. You'll see the Docket Genie interface with five main tabs

## Using Docket Genie

### Step 1: Connect to PACER

1. Click on the **Connect** tab
2. Enter your PACER credentials:
   - **Username**: Your PACER account username
   - **Password**: Your PACER account password
   - **Client Code**: (Optional) If you have a client billing code
3. Read the fee disclaimer
4. Click **Connect to PACER**

**Important**: Your credentials are NOT stored. They are only used for the current session.

#### Session Management

- PACER sessions typically last 1 hour
- A timer displays your remaining session time
- When your session expires, you'll be prompted to reconnect
- You can manually disconnect at any time

### Step 2: Search for Cases

After connecting, you'll automatically be moved to the **Search** tab.

#### Search Criteria

You can search by any combination of:

- **Case Number**: e.g., 1:23-cv-12345
- **Court**: Select from dropdown (District, Bankruptcy, or Appellate)
- **Party Name**: Plaintiff or defendant name
- **Attorney Name**: Attorney representing a party
- **Case Title**: e.g., "Smith v. Jones"
- **Filing Date Range**: From and to dates

#### Running a Search

1. Enter at least one search criterion
2. Click **Search Cases**
3. Results will display with:
   - Case title and number
   - Court and judge
   - Filing date and status
   - Case type and nature
4. Each result shows the estimated PACER fee

#### Working with Search Results

Each case in the results has two actions:

- **View Docket**: See all docket entries for the case
- **Details**: View comprehensive case information

### Step 3: View Docket Reports

When you click **View Docket** on a case:

1. You're taken to the **Docket** tab
2. Case header displays:
   - Case title and number
   - Court, judge, filing date, status
   - Total entries and estimated fee
3. Docket entries are listed chronologically

#### Understanding Docket Entries

Each entry shows:

- **Entry Number**: Sequential number
- **Date Filed**: When the document was filed
- **Description**: Type of filing (Motion, Order, etc.)
- **Docket Text**: Full description of the entry
- **Filed By**: Party who filed the document
- **Associated Documents**: Downloadable documents

#### Expanding Entries

- Click on any entry to expand it
- View full docket text and associated documents
- Download documents directly from expanded view

### Step 4: View Case Details

When you click **Details** on a case:

1. You're taken to the **Details** tab
2. Comprehensive information includes:

#### Case Overview
- Case number, type, and status
- Filing date and court
- Judge and magistrate judge
- Nature of suit and jurisdiction
- Cause of action and demand amount

#### Parties
- All plaintiffs, defendants, and other parties
- Party type and role
- Legal representation information

#### Attorneys
- Attorney names and bar numbers
- Law firm and contact information
- Party representation

#### Case Statistics
- Total docket entries
- Total documents
- Number of parties and attorneys
- Last activity date

### Step 5: Download and Manage Documents

#### Downloading Documents

1. From any docket entry, click **Download** on a document
2. The document is downloaded and added to the **Documents** tab
3. You'll see:
   - Document filename
   - Case number
   - Number of pages
   - PACER fee charged

#### Managing Downloaded Documents

Go to the **Documents** tab to:

1. **View All Downloads**: See all documents you've downloaded
2. **Select Documents**: Use checkboxes to select multiple documents
3. **Delete Documents**: Remove documents you no longer need
4. **Track Costs**: See total PACER fees for downloaded documents

#### Analyzing Documents with AI

The powerful feature of Docket Genie is AI integration:

1. Click **Analyze with AI** on any downloaded document
2. You're redirected to the Document Analysis tool
3. The document is pre-loaded and ready for AI analysis
4. Ask questions about the document
5. Get AI-powered insights and summaries

## PACER Fees and Billing

### Understanding PACER Fees

PACER charges fees for most activities:

- **Search Results**: $0.10 per page
- **Docket Reports**: $0.10 per page, capped at $3.00
- **Document Downloads**: $0.10 per page, capped at $3.00 per document

### Fee Display

Docket Genie shows estimated fees BEFORE you perform actions:

- Search results show total estimated fee
- Docket reports display estimated cost
- Document downloads show per-page cost

### Billing

- All fees are billed directly to your PACER account
- Docket Genie does NOT charge additional fees
- You'll receive your regular PACER quarterly bill
- No deduction from AI Wizard Service Credits

### Fee Exemptions

Some PACER users qualify for fee exemptions or waivers. Contact PACER directly for information about:

- Pro bono attorney exemptions
- Low-volume user fee waivers
- Educational or research exemptions

## Tips and Best Practices

### Effective Searching

1. **Be Specific**: More specific searches return fewer, more relevant results
2. **Use Case Numbers**: If you know the case number, use it for fastest results
3. **Select Court**: Narrow by court to reduce results and fees
4. **Date Ranges**: Use date ranges to focus on recent cases

### Managing Costs

1. **Review Estimates**: Always check estimated fees before proceeding
2. **Download Strategically**: Only download documents you need
3. **Use Case Details**: Free case details can help you decide what to download
4. **Track Your Usage**: Monitor downloads in the Documents tab

### Session Management

1. **Plan Your Work**: Complete related tasks in one session
2. **Monitor Time**: Keep an eye on the session timer
3. **Reconnect Promptly**: Reconnect before session expires to avoid interruption
4. **Disconnect When Done**: Manually disconnect to free PACER resources

### AI Analysis

1. **Analyze Multiple Documents**: Download several, then analyze together
2. **Ask Specific Questions**: Use the AI to find specific information quickly
3. **Generate Summaries**: Get AI-powered case summaries
4. **Compare Documents**: Analyze multiple documents from the same case

## Troubleshooting

### Cannot Connect to PACER

**Problem**: Authentication fails or error message appears

**Solutions**:
1. Verify your PACER username and password
2. Check that your PACER account is active
3. Ensure you have no outstanding PACER bills
4. Try logging in directly to pacer.uscourts.gov to verify credentials
5. Contact PACER support if issues persist

### Session Expired

**Problem**: "Session expired" message appears

**Solutions**:
1. Click the **Reconnect** button
2. Re-enter your PACER credentials
3. Your work is saved; you can continue where you left off

### Search Returns No Results

**Problem**: Search completes but finds no cases

**Solutions**:
1. Verify your search criteria are correct
2. Try broader search terms
3. Check date ranges (are they too narrow?)
4. Try searching different courts
5. Verify case number format (include district code)

### Document Won't Download

**Problem**: Download button is disabled or fails

**Solutions**:
1. Check if document is marked as "restricted" or "sealed"
2. Verify your PACER session is still active
3. Check if you have sufficient PACER account credit
4. Some documents may require special access permissions

### Can't Access Docket Genie

**Problem**: Don't see Docket Genie in navigation

**Solutions**:
1. Verify you're logged in as an attorney
2. Client accounts cannot access Docket Genie
3. Contact support if you should have attorney access

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a separate PACER account?

Yes. Docket Genie connects to your existing PACER account. If you don't have one, register at pacer.uscourts.gov.

### Will this cost me money?

Yes, standard PACER fees apply ($0.10 per page). These fees are billed by PACER, not AI Wizard.

### Can I analyze documents without downloading?

No. Documents must be downloaded to your Documents tab before AI analysis. This ensures you have access to the full document for comprehensive analysis.

### What courts are supported?

All federal courts accessible through PACER:
- U.S. District Courts
- U.S. Bankruptcy Courts
- U.S. Courts of Appeals
- Supreme Court

### Can I save my searches?

Currently, search results are session-based. Downloaded documents persist until you delete them.

### What happens to my downloaded documents?

Documents remain in the Documents tab until you manually delete them. They're also available for AI analysis at any time.

### Can clients access Docket Genie?

No. Docket Genie is exclusively for attorney accounts due to PACER's access requirements and fee structure.

### Is there a limit on searches or downloads?

No limits from AI Wizard. PACER's own limits and fees apply. Heavy users may want to review PACER's bulk data options.
